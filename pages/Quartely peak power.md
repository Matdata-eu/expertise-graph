description:: The Flemish household electricity cost is settled based on energy consumption as well as maximum power use. The maximum power is a yearly value based on the average monthly values. The average monthly value is the maximum 15min average power consumption. Yes.. better read that twice. In order to avoid overcharging, the home automation system interfaces with the DSMR reader to calculate this 15min value and regulate power consumption where possible.
