description:: The pantograph is that part of the traction vehicle in a train that conducts electrical current to the engine (or battery). It has a certain dimension and as it is on top of the vehicle, is heavily influenced by the sway vibrations of the vehicle and external influences such as wind. In order to design the overhead structures, it is important to keep everything out of the path of the pantograph. The possible positions of the pantograph are called the "free space". This projects goal was to revise the design principles used in the enterprise and participate in the revision of the principles on a European level.
