- Workshop goals (from ERA contract ToR):
	- Clarify RINF expectations, rules, and validation logic.
	- Identify common issues faced by Infrastructure Managers.
	- Improve alignment between operational location data and the ERA ontology.
	- Provide practical technical guidance for higher quality RINF submissions.
- Delivery scope:
	- Co-designed agenda and content with ERA, including demonstrations and real/anonymised examples.
	- Facilitated Q&A and hands-on exercises during the workshop session.
	- Produced post-workshop technical recommendations.
- Repository contribution:
	- Maintained Slidev workshop materials, participant getting-started guide, and publish/export automation (GitHub Pages + PDF export).
